# What we need from the gym owner

Everything below replaces placeholders in `data/site.ts` and `public/images/`.

## Brand
- [ ] Gym name (and legal/registered name)
- [ ] Logo — SVG or high-res PNG (transparent background)
- [ ] Brand colour (hex code) and any brand fonts
- [ ] Tagline / headline they want on the hero

## Business details
- [ ] Full address (+ Google Maps pin / PIN code)
- [ ] Phone number and **WhatsApp number** that should receive trial bookings
- [ ] Email address
- [ ] Opening hours (weekdays, weekends, holidays)
- [ ] Instagram, Facebook and YouTube links
- [ ] Google Business Profile link, current rating and review count
- [ ] Domain name (and who controls the DNS)

## Pricing & offers
- [ ] Final prices for Monthly, Quarterly and Yearly plans (incl. GST?)
- [ ] What each plan includes (PT sessions, freeze days, guest passes, etc.)
- [ ] Any joining fee, launch offer or referral discount

## Programs & timetable
- [ ] Final list of programs with timings
- [ ] Weekly class schedule (class name, time, trainer)
- [ ] Free-trial policy (how long, what's included, ID requirements)

## People
- [ ] 4 trainers: name, specialisation, years of experience, certifications, short bio, Instagram handle
- [ ] Professional trainer photos (portrait, ~800×1000, good lighting)
- [ ] Written consent from trainers to be featured

## Photos & proof
- [ ] 6–10 gym photos (equipment floor, studio, reception, exterior)
- [ ] 3+ member transformations: **before/after photos with the same pose and lighting**, name, duration, result — with **signed written consent**
- [ ] 6–10 genuine Google reviews (reviewer name, rating, text) — copy accurately, don't invent

## Operational
- [ ] Where should booking requests go besides WhatsApp? (email address / Google Sheet)
- [ ] Who will respond to enquiries, and how quickly?
- [ ] Privacy-policy / terms text if they want it linked in the footer
